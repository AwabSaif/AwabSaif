## <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Who_I_Am-007ACC?style=flat-square&logo=user&logoColor=white" alt="Who I Am" /> Who I Am

I am a dedicated **Software Engineer** specializing in architecting and developing scalable, data-driven web applications. My core technical focus centers on **Angular**, **C# .NET 8**, **Next.js**, and building distributed **Microservices**. I also have a strong, established background in the **MERN Stack** (MongoDB, Express, React, Node.js). 

Beyond standard web development, I have specialized experience in **Earth Observation (EO) and Geospatial Engineering**. I was a key contributor in building an Earth Observation platform, where I focused heavily on integrating advanced map services (Mapbox, MapLibre, Google Maps). I have a strong command of engineering custom interactive geospatial tools and utilizing tile servers like TiTiler to process and effectively render high-resolution satellite imagery.

**What I Do:**
- <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Architecture-339933?style=flat-square&logo=server&logoColor=white" alt="Architecture" /> **Microservices & Backend Architecture**: Designing and implementing scalable, high-performance server-side solutions using C# .NET and Node.js.
- <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Frontend-FF6B9D?style=flat-square&logo=angular&logoColor=white" alt="Frontend" /> **Advanced UI Development**: Creating responsive, maintainable, and accessible design systems utilizing Angular, Next.js, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS.
- <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Geospatial-4ECDC4?style=flat-square&logo=map&logoColor=white" alt="Geospatial" /> **Earth Observation & Geospatial Solutions**: Highly capable in integrating map services (Mapbox, MapLibre), building advanced drawing tools, and working closely with tile servers (TiTiler) for satellite imagery rendering.
- <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Full_Stack-007ACC?style=flat-square&logo=code&logoColor=white" alt="Full Stack" /> **End-to-End Delivery**: Bridging the gap between intuitive user interfaces and robust backend systems to deliver complete, production-ready software.
